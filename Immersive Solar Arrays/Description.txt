[h1]Immersive Solar Arrays[/h1]

Harvest solar power for the needs of your base. Wire car batteries into a battery bank,
put panels on the roof, and run your fridges and freezers off the sun instead of a
generator that has to be fed.

This is a fork of RadX5 and Poltergeist's Immersive Solar Arrays, rebuilt for build 42.
Support for build 41 has been dropped.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

Build 42.20 or newer.

[b][URL=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2969455858]Target Square: On Load Commands[/URL][/b] is needed for the pre-placed crates that
spawn around the map. Without it the rest of the mod still works, you just do not get
those crates. It is safe to add or remove on an existing save.

[h1]How it works[/h1]

Wire a car battery, build a battery bank, then craft panels and connect them to the bank.
The bank powers everything a generator would, in the same radius, and charges during the
day from whatever panels it can see. Cloud, fog, temperature and the length of the day
all move the number.

Right click the bank for its status window. The summary tab tells you whether the panels
are keeping up, and how long the charge lasts at the current draw. The details tab lists
what is actually pulling power.

A generator plugged in near the bank becomes its backup. With a failsafe trigger on the
generator's square, the bank starts the generator when it runs low and stops it once it
has caught up.

Everything above works in both singleplayer and multiplayer.

[h1]Options[/h1]

Sandbox options cover panel efficiency, how quickly batteries wear out, DIY battery
capacity, how often the bank charges, how the power draw is calculated, loot rarity for
each item group, world spawns and stash houses.

Two of the recipes, making a solar panel and making an inverter from scratch, are off by
default. Turn on expanded recipes in the sandbox options if you want them.

[h1]Notes[/h1]

Only run one version of this mod. The mod id is "ISA".

Some options need a game reload, and stash houses want a new save to appear properly.

Test the mod on a save you are willing to lose before adding it to a long running one.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]

Reproduce it with only this mod enabled and write down the steps. If it only happens with
other mods loaded, narrow it down to which ones. Check the console log for errors and post
those along with everything else.
